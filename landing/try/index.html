ShieldBot extension demo

Three transactions on Robinhood Chain (4663), one per outcome, so the firewall can be watched deciding. Load the extension unpacked first: see the judge guide.
not connected chain unknown
Nothing is spent. ShieldBot decides before the wallet is asked, so declining in its overlay ends the request there. If you press Proceed Anyway it reaches your wallet, where you should reject it.

1. Block recommended

An unlimited approval granted to a plain address rather than a contract, on a token the launch watch blocked.

Expect Do not proceed with this transaction, with a drainer pattern and an unlimited approval among the signals.

2. Unknown, never safe

A transfer to a token whose sell behaviour the scan could not settle.

Expect Unknown, naming what could not be checked, rather than a clean result.

3. No major risks

A transfer to the burn address, which holds no contract and grants no approval.

Expect No major risks detected.
