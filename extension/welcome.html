ShieldAI

Checks your transactions on 8 EVM chains before you sign: Ethereum, BNB Chain, opBNB, Base, Arbitrum, Polygon, Optimism and Robinhood Chain.

01
Analyze
Transactions and signature requests a page sends to your wallet are checked for honeypots, rug pulls and drainer contracts before you confirm. Sends you start in your wallet's own window are not checked.
02
Simulate
When a transaction can be simulated, see which tokens leave and enter your wallet before you confirm.
03
Warn
High-risk transactions get a clear warning before you sign, and you choose whether to cancel or continue. If a request times out, or your wallet's chain cannot be identified or differs from the chain the transaction names, the request is refused so you can retry. Known phishing sites are flagged when you open them.
